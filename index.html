Drafts for the owner's TikTok inbox

SingKshop Inbox is a small helper for the @singkshop TikTok account. It sends a photo post (one image, or a short carousel) and a caption into the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft.

The owner then opens that inbox item in the TikTok app, checks the post, and publishes it themselves if they want to. This app does not publish publicly and has no “post now” button.

Who it is for. Only the owner of the SingKshop account. It is not a public scheduling product and it does not upload to other people's accounts.

What it stores. Sign-in tokens are kept on the owner's own computer. This website only hosts public pages and the image files TikTok needs to fetch.

Questions. Email aorealme.ai@gmail.com.

How a draft is sent

  1. The owner signs in with TikTok and allows draft upload only (not direct publishing).
  2. A prepared photo and caption are uploaded to the owner's inbox.
  3. TikTok sends an inbox notification. The owner reviews the post in the TikTok app and decides whether to publish.
