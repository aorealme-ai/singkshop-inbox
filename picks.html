How to pick a safe charger

This section goes with the @singkshop post on how to pick a safe charger in Singapore. The takeaway: any charger that passes these checks is fine — you don't need this exact product. Look for the SAFETY Mark, check the number on the official register, and match the charger to your device.

Anker Prime Charger A2687 (TikTok Shop)

Why this one: a 3-port 160W GaN wall charger with the Singapore SAFETY Mark (I checked it on the CPSO register, 16 Nov 2026). Official product page (not an affiliate link): Anker Prime Charger 160W (A2687) on anker.com.sg.
