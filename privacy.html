Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

This policy explains what SingKshop Inbox collects and how it is used. The app is only for the owner of the @singkshop TikTok account.

What we collect

When the owner signs in with TikTok, TikTok may share a user identifier and tokens that let the app upload a draft to that same account. The app also holds the photos and captions the owner chooses to send.

Where it is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a locked-down local file. They are not stored on this website, and they are not uploaded to a cloud account we control. This website may host public pages (including these policy pages) and the image files needed so TikTok can fetch a photo from a verified URL.

How it is used

Tokens are used only to send a draft to the owner's TikTok inbox and to check the status of that upload. We do not sell data. We do not use the data for ads. We do not sign in to other people's TikTok accounts.

Sharing

Photos are fetched by TikTok from the public image URL when a draft is uploaded. We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

Retention

Tokens stay on the owner's computer until they delete them or they expire. The owner can also disconnect the app in their TikTok settings. Public image files stay on this site until the owner removes them.

Contact

For privacy questions, or to ask us to delete something we control, message @singkshop on TikTok or open an issue on GitHub.
