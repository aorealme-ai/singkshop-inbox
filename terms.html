These terms cover the SingKshop Inbox helper (“the app”), operated by the owner of the SingKshop TikTok account. The app sends photo drafts to that owner's own TikTok inbox so they can review and post in the TikTok app. It does not publish posts on its own.

Who may use it

The app is for the owner of the @singkshop account only. If you are not that owner, you should not sign in or grant permissions.

What the app does

With the owner's permission, the app can upload a photo (or a short set of photos) and a caption to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft. The owner must open the TikTok notification and choose whether to publish. The app will not request permission to post directly to a public profile.

Your responsibilities

The owner is responsible for the content of each draft, for following TikTok's terms and community rules, and for any post they choose to publish from the inbox. Image files placed on this site are public URLs that TikTok may fetch.
